---
title: "Deleting and Disabling Fields"
icon: "eye-off"
description: "Core fields can only be disabled, which keeps their data but takes them off forms, tables, Pages and Destinations; a custom field can be deleted permanently, stranding its stored values."
---

There are two ways to get a field off your Items, and which one you get depends on the kind of
field. Core fields can only be disabled. Custom fields are deleted outright.

Both actions happen on the chip strip in the Collection's settings, under **Fields**, and
neither takes effect until you press **Save changes**.

## Disabling a core field

Name, Item ID, Description, and Keywords cannot be deleted — the × on the chip disables them
instead. Disabling is a display decision, not a data one:

- the field disappears from the Item form and the Items table,
- every value already stored stays exactly where it is,
- its Required flag is cleared, so re-enabling brings it back optional,
- its default value stops being applied to new Items,
- you can turn it back on any time from **Add More Fields**, values intact.

Disabling also takes the field away from your Pages and Destinations. While a core field is
off, its stored values are left out of the data they read: a Page section bound to it shows
nothing, a Destination URL built from it no longer resolves (the scan moves on to the next
conditional rule, or shows "This link isn't ready yet"), and a condition referencing it
evaluates to `false` with no error shown. Switching off Item ID, for instance, stops every
Destination URL that uses `{{item.item_id}}`. Turn the field back on and its values are used
again.

## Deleting a custom field

A custom field's chip carries a trash icon, and it means it. QRtub asks you to confirm, with
two warnings:

- **Page templates and destination links that reference this field will show broken or
  unresolved bindings.** Nothing rewrites them for you the way a
  [rename](/fields/renaming-a-field) does.
- **Existing item data stored for this field will become inaccessible.** The values leave the
  Items table, the Item form and every CSV export.

Creating a new field with the same key afterward does not recover them. A new field gets a new
internal storage ID, so it starts empty — the old values stay stranded under the ID that was
deleted. Treat deletion as one-way.

Deleting a field does not delete any Items. They stay, minus that column.

## Which to reach for

If you might want the data later, do not delete. Nothing stops you from leaving a field enabled
and simply not filling it in, and a Collection carries no per-field cost for that.

Custom fields that arrive from a Collection template or the **Add More Fields** library can sit
in a disabled state, and those you can re-enable freely. But once a custom field is on the chip
strip, the only removal action offered for it is Delete — there is no "hide this custom field"
switch.

## Before you delete

1. Search your Destinations and Page sections for the field's key and remove or repoint those
   bindings. A Destination URL left pointing at a deleted field no longer resolves, so the
   scan silently moves on to the next conditional rule or shows "This link isn't ready yet".
2. Export the Collection's Items to CSV first if the values are worth keeping. That export is
   the only copy you will have.
3. Then delete, and **Save changes**.
