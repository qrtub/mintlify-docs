## Notes / things checked with zero findings

The following American-spelling patterns from the brief were searched for
(base word plus common inflections) and **do not occur** anywhere in the
scoped files, so no rows are listed for them: `favorite`, `license` (as noun),
`catalog`, `gray`, `defense`/`offense`, `traveled`/`traveling`, `canceled`/
`canceling` (the one hit, "cancelled", is already correct), `fulfill`, `meter`/
`metre` (no distance-unit or measuring-device mentions at all), `labor`,
`flavor`, `humor`, `rumor`, `armor`, `vapor`, `odor`, `tumor`, `harbor`,
`mold`, `plow`, `tire`/`tyre`, `curb`/`kerb`, `jewelry`, `enroll`/`enrol`,
`skillful`/`willful`, and `-yze`/`-yse` verbs (e.g. `analyze`, `paralyze`).

Two matches were skipped because they are already correct AU/UK spelling,
not defects: `help/app-links.mdx:11` ("recognises") and
`help/app-links.mdx:35` ("cancelled").

Two occurrences of `organizationName` (`help/conditional-visibility.mdx:162`
and the code-column cell in `help/using-fields.mdx:85`) are literal API/field
identifiers inside backticks and were deliberately left unflagged — only the
prose description text next to them was flagged.

`program` and `judgment`/`judgement` were excluded per the task brief (both
are standard/acceptable in Australian English). Instances of "practice" (noun)
and "check" (verb) were reviewed and are spelled correctly for AU English as
used (no "practise"-as-noun or "cheque"-for-check confusion found). "Coordination"
was reviewed and is an accepted modern AU/UK spelling without the hyphen, so
not flagged.
