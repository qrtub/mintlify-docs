## 3. Does Key Concepts fully answer the question?

Not directly. It never uses the word "expire" and never states a policy for Link
lifetime, inactivity timeouts, or what happens to Links if a subscription lapses or is
cancelled. What it *does* say, which bears strongly on the question by implication:

> "Links are digital patterns—the URLs that QR codes resolve to. They exist
> independently from Items... Reassign Links if you make mistakes—no reprinting
> needed... Change where Links point without touching physical QR codes."

> "The core benefit: Change where your QR codes point at any time without touching
> the physical codes."

> "Each entity has its own lifecycle. The excavator might be sold. The Link persists
> and can be reassigned. The metal plaque might outlast the excavator and get moved to
> new equipment."

These sentences imply Links are designed to be permanent/reassignable rather than
time-limited — but this is inference from marketing-style framing about *reprinting*
and *reassignment*, not an explicit statement that "Links never expire" or any
discussion of edge cases like account cancellation, non-payment, or long-term
inactivity. The question as posed ("do they ever expire") most naturally includes
those SaaS-lifecycle scenarios, which the page is silent on.

## 4. Any other page that would help?

- **Physical Media Management Basics** and **Print Batches** discuss *Media*
  durability (vinyl stickers 1–3 years, metal plaques 10+ years, billboards 6
  months–3 years) and a "Retired" deployment status — but these describe the physical
  material's lifespan or an operator's manual bookkeeping choice, not whether the
  underlying digital Link stops resolving. A careless reader (human or AI) could
  conflate "vinyl sticker durability: 1-3 years" with "the Link/QR code expires after
  1-3 years," which would be a misreading of the source material.
- The homepage repeats the "Print Once. Update Forever" tagline, reinforcing the
  no-expiration framing, but again never addresses billing/inactivity-driven expiry.
- No page in the index discusses account cancellation, non-payment, or dormancy
  effects on Links at all.
