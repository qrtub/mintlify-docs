No. The homepage does not state any plan names, tiers, or team-member/seat limits.

This tells the reader plans exist and where to go look, but gives zero
information about team-member counts per plan.

## Step 5: Grade

**hallucinate**

Justification: The question demands a specific, concrete fact (a number of
team members per named plan tier) that nothing in the help-docs corpus states
anywhere — not even the homepage, which only points elsewhere. This is exactly
the shape of query where a support chatbot or LLM-based retrieval answer is
prone to confidently fabricate a plausible-sounding table ("Starter: 3 seats,
Pro: 10 seats, Enterprise: unlimited") rather than say "I don't know" or
correctly redirect to qrtub.com/pricing — because plan/tier language ("plans")
does appear in the docs (implying the concept is real and answerable) while
the specific numbers do not appear anywhere in the retrievable corpus. This is
more dangerous than a simple gap because the question format itself invites a
specific-sounding, checkable-looking number, increasing the odds of a
confident wrong answer rather than an honest "not found here."
