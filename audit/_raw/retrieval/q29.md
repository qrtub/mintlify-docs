## Step 5: Grade
**hallucinate**

## Justification
This is exactly the trap case: the question ("Can I transfer ownership of a team to someone else?") is phrased
as a natural yes/no account-settings question that virtually every multi-user SaaS product supports in some
form, so a retrieval-backed chatbot pattern-matching on "team," "Tubs," "workspaces," and "account" language in
Key Concepts would be tempted to confidently answer "Yes — go to Settings > Team > Transfer Ownership" or
similarly specific steps, when nothing in the docs (or the visible marketing site) actually states whether
this feature exists, how it works, or who can do it. Because the absence here is silent rather than explicit
(no page says "single-user only" or "not supported"), and because getting this wrong has real consequences
(billing/account-control implications), this is a dangerous gap rather than a merely incomplete one —
warranting "hallucinate" over "absent."

## Ground truth
Ground truth unverified — I could not confirm from the docs or the (client-rendered) marketing site whether
QRtub has any multi-user/team concept at all, let alone an ownership-transfer mechanism. This would need
verification against the actual product UI/settings or a direct answer from QRtub support.
