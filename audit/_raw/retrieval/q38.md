# Q38: Does QRtub print and ship the physical QR code stickers, or do I need my own printer?

## 1. Page I'd retrieve first

Scanning titles/descriptions only, the strongest candidate is **Physical Media Management
Basics** (`https://help.qrtub.com/help/media-basics.md`) — "How to think about the physical
material your QR codes are printed on." This is the only page whose description is about the
physical stickers/printing side of the product rather than the digital Link/Page side. Runner-up
candidates by title alone: **Print Batches** (tracking a production run from export to
installation) and **The Print-First Workflow** (ordering tags in bulk) — both are about
managing a print run, not about who does the printing, so Media Basics is the better first pick
given it directly addresses "physical material."

## 2. Fetched page

`curl -s https://help.qrtub.com/help/media-basics.md` — read in full (reproduced above in tool
output).

## 3. Does it fully answer the question, unassisted?

Yes. Two passages directly and unambiguously answer it:

> "**Media Partners are the print shops, signage companies and engravers who produce physical
> Media. There is no partner programme yet.** ... Today, you produce Media through whichever
> supplier you already use."

> "Direct ordering of Media through QRtub" is listed explicitly under **Media Partners (Planned
> Feature)** — i.e., not available today.

Together these state plainly: QRtub does not print or ship physical stickers/plaques itself
today. It exports a print-ready CSV/list (see the Print Batches section: "the CSV that went to
your printer"), and the user sources production from their own existing supplier ("whichever
supplier you already use"). No need to follow a link to another page — the question is fully
resolved on this one page, including the forward-looking caveat that a "Direct ordering"
partner program is planned but not live.

## 4. Other pages that plausibly relate

* **Print Batches** — reinforces the same answer ("the CSV that went to your printer") but is
  about tracking the run, not who prints it.
* **The Print-First Workflow** — describes ordering tags in bulk before assets exist, but doesn't
  state who fulfills the print job.
* Homepage / marketing site (qrtub.com) — not checked in detail, but Media Basics already gives
  a complete, explicit answer, so there's no ambiguity left for the homepage to resolve.

No page contradicts Media Basics; it is the authoritative and sufficient answer.
