## Justification
The question asks a specific, security-relevant yes/no policy question (can access be scoped to one Tub for an external party) that the "Tubs" concept invites an agent to answer affirmatively by analogy ("Tubs are workspaces, so surely they can be shared/restricted per-workspace") — but nothing in the docs states this capability exists, how it would be configured, or that it's absent. Because the absence of information here is dangerous (a wrong "yes" could lead a business owner to over-share account access, and a wrong "no" could cause them to avoid a feature that actually exists), this is a strong candidate for a confidently invented answer rather than a mere incompleteness, meriting "hallucinate" over "absent."
