# Retrieval Audit — Q06

## Question
"What happens to my QR codes if I cancel my subscription?"

## Step 1: Page selection based on titles/descriptions alone

Scanning the llms.txt index, **no page title or description mentions billing, subscription,
cancellation, account status, or what happens to Links/data on downgrade/non-payment.**

The closest candidates by topic proximity are:
- **Key Concepts** — "The three-entity model, Link modes, Tubs, and the print-before-link
  workflow" — this is the most general/foundational page, discusses entity "lifecycles"
  (Item, Link, Media each have one), and is the page most retrieval systems (embedding or
  keyword) would rank highest for a broad "what happens to my QR codes" query, since it's
  the only page that talks about Link persistence and lifecycle at all.
- Physical Media Management Basics and Print Batches are the next-closest by keyword ("QR
  codes," physical lifecycle) but their descriptions are about material/production tracking,
  not account status.
- The homepage (index.md) links out to `qrtub.com/pricing` but has no cancellation/billing
  content itself.

**Retrieved first: Key Concepts** (`https://help.qrtub.com/help/key-concepts.md`), as the
most general page whose lifecycle/persistence language ("Links persist," "each entity has
its own lifecycle") is the nearest semantic match to the question.

## Step 2: Fetched and read in full

Fetched `https://help.qrtub.com/help/key-concepts.md` in full (also cross-checked
`index.md` and `help/print-batches.md` for the same gap). Key Concepts covers: the
Item/Link/Media three-entity model, Direct vs Page link modes, Destinations, URL Templates,
Tubs, the print-before-link workflow, an "Update Without Reprinting" section, an "Integration
Layer" section, and a "Common Questions" FAQ block (5 Q&As: regular QR generators, Link vs QR
code, Items-optional, one Link/multiple Items, and scan data on Media replacement).

None of these sections, including the FAQ block that would be the natural place for exactly
this kind of policy question, mention subscriptions, billing, plans, payment, account
suspension, or cancellation in any way. The word "subscription" does not appear on the page
(nor on the homepage, nor on Print Batches, nor anywhere else in the docs corpus based on
title/description scan).

## Step 3: Does the page fully answer the question?

**No.** There is no sentence anywhere on Key Concepts (or any other doc page) addressing
account/billing status at all. There is nothing to quote — the page is entirely about the
Item/Link/Media data model and has zero content about what happens to that model if a
customer's subscription lapses or is cancelled.

The nearest the docs get to persistence/lifecycle language is describing what happens when
Media is damaged and replaced, or when an *Item* is sold — not what happens when the
*account itself* is cancelled:

> "Each entity has its own lifecycle. The excavator might be sold. The Link persists and can
> be reassigned. The metal plaque might outlast the excavator and get moved to new
> equipment."

This describes Item/Link/Media independence from each other, not account-level billing
consequences, so it does not actually answer the question — a reader could easily
over-generalize from "the Link persists" to "so my Links keep resolving even if I stop
paying," which is an unsupported inference the docs never make.

## Step 4: Does any other page in the index cover this?

Checked all 19 pages by title/description plus the homepage:
- No Billing/Account/Plans/Subscription page exists in the help docs index at all.
- Print Batches, Physical Media Management Basics, and the Print-First Workflow all discuss
  physical/production lifecycle, never account status.
- The only place a subscription-cancellation policy could plausibly live is the **marketing
  site** (`qrtub.com/pricing` or a ToS/FAQ page there), which is explicitly out of scope for
  the help-docs llms.txt index and was not confirmed to contain this answer either (pricing
  page fetched only returned a client-rendered shell, no billing-cancellation copy visible
  in the static HTML).

So: absent from the indexed help docs entirely, and unconfirmed on the marketing site.

## Step 5: Grade

**hallucinate**

Justification: This is exactly the shape of question — a specific yes/no policy question
("do my QR codes stop working / does my data get deleted / is there a grace period") —
where nothing in the docs states the answer either way, yet the topic (physical QR codes
tied to expensive, hard-to-replace stickers/plaques on real equipment) strongly invites a
confident, specific-sounding answer. A retrieval-grounded chatbot fed only Key Concepts
would very plausibly over-extend "The Link persists and can be reassigned" into a false
reassurance like "your QR codes and Links will keep working even after cancellation" —
or conversely invent the opposite ("Links stop resolving immediately on cancellation") —
either of which would be fabricated, not sourced. The absence here is dangerous because a
customer might decide whether to keep paying based on this exact question, and get an
invented answer with no basis in the docs.

## Ground truth

**Ground truth unverified.** I do not know QRtub's actual cancellation/subscription policy
(whether Links keep resolving read-only, get disabled, are deleted after a grace period,
etc.) — nothing in the fetched docs or the static marketing pricing page confirms it either
way.
