# Retrieval Audit — Q07

## Question
"How do I change where a QR code points after it's already printed and stuck on equipment?"

## Step 1: Page selected from titles/descriptions alone
Given only the index of titles/descriptions, the strongest candidate is **Key Concepts**
(https://help.qrtub.com/help/key-concepts.md) — its description explicitly promises "the
three-entity model, Link modes, Tubs, and the print-before-link workflow," which is the
conceptual home for "Links exist independently of Items and can be reassigned." A retrieval
system might also reasonably pull **The Print-First Workflow** (description: "apply them as
gear arrives, and connect them when you are ready") since the question's framing — code
already printed and stuck on equipment — matches that page's premise almost word for word.
Neither title uses the words "edit," "update," "change," or "reassign" directly, so this is
an imperfect match at the title level; **Creating Your First Link** is a plausible third
guess (it mentions "connect it to an Item") but is about the initial creation flow, not
changing an existing connection.

I retrieved **Key Concepts** first as the primary candidate, then cross-checked **The
Print-First Workflow**, **Creating Your First Link**, and the homepage to see if any of them
supplied the missing procedural detail.

## Step 2 & 3: Full text review — does it answer completely?

### Key Concepts
Directly relevant passages:

> "Reassign Links if you make mistakes—no reprinting needed"
> "Change where Links point without touching physical QR codes"

And under "Update Without Reprinting":

> "**The core benefit:** Change where your QR codes point at any time without touching the
> physical codes."
> "**Broken links** - URL changed or site migrated → Update the Destination"
> "Example: 500 equipment QR codes point to "System A" for inspections. Two years later, you
> switch to "System B." With traditional QR codes, you reprint 500 codes. With QRtub, you
> update 500 Destinations. Physical codes unchanged."

This confirms the capability exists and explains the underlying mechanism (Destinations are
edited, not the physical code) but it never states the actual procedure — no mention of
where in the product you go to edit a Link's Destination, what button to click, or whether
you edit it on the Link itself, the Item, or the Page. It's a conceptual/marketing-style
answer, not a how-to.

### The Print-First Workflow (checked because it matches the question's premise even more
closely — "already printed and stuck on equipment")

> "Tags get put on the wrong machine. Gear gets sold. A Link can be reassigned to a different
> Item at any point, so a mistake costs an edit rather than a reprint."
> "Because the destination is held by QRtub rather than baked into the code, you can change
> where every tag points later — a new system, a new URL structure — without touching
> anything physical."

Same pattern: confirms the "yes, and here's why it's possible" answer, but again gives no
concrete steps (no "go to the Link's detail page and edit the Destination field" instructions).

### Creating Your First Link
Only covers generating a *new* Link and connecting it the first time (Steps 1–4: Navigate to
Links → Generate → Choose Type → Print or Connect). Nothing about editing an existing Link's
Destination after the fact.

### Homepage (index.md)
Same marketing-level claim ("Update Destinations whenever requirements change... update the
Destination. Don't reprint 500 stickers") with no procedural detail either.

## Step 4: Does any other page in the index fully answer it?
No. Across Key Concepts, The Print-First Workflow, Creating Your First Link, and the
homepage, the message is consistent and repeated: this is possible, it's the product's core
value proposition, and it's done by editing the "Destination" rather than the physical code.
But not one page tells the user the actual click path — e.g., "open the Link from the Links
list, click Edit, change the Destination URL, save." There's no dedicated "Editing a Link" or
"Managing Links" page in the index at all — a real gap in the documentation set.

## Step 5: Grade
**partial**

The capability is confirmed clearly and repeatedly (yes, you can do this, and the docs even
explain *why* it works — Destinations are decoupled from the physical code), so this is not
"absent," and there's no invented/wrong fact being stated (so not "hallucinate"). But the
actual question — "how do I" — is left unanswered: no page gives the concrete UI steps for
editing an existing Link's Destination. A user would come away certain the feature exists but
without knowing where to click.

## Step 6: Ground truth
Ground truth unverified — I have not confirmed against the live QRtub app what the actual UI
flow for editing a Link's Destination looks like (e.g., whether you edit it from the Links
list, the Item detail page, or a Page editor). The docs' silence on this procedural step is
itself the finding, independent of what the true flow is.

## One-line justification
The docs consistently confirm that changing a Link's Destination after printing is possible
and explain the concept, but no page anywhere in the index gives the actual step-by-step UI
instructions to do it — leaving the literal "how do I" unanswered.
