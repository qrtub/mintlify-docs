## Step 3: Does it fully answer the question, no further page needed?

Yes. The page states directly, in two places:

> "Back in the Tub, you can filter Items by the batch they were printed in — useful when a run
> needs replacing, or when you want to know what a particular delivery covered."

and, describing the export/creation step:

> "The export downloads a CSV **and creates a batch**. This is worth knowing: exporting is what
> marks those links as printed."

Together with the "Finding your batches" section ("Batches live under **Access Media** in the main
navigation. Each row shows the batch name, status, when it was printed and how many Items and links
it contains.") and the per-code status tracking section, this fully explains how to find which batch
a given QR code/link belongs to and how to trace it — no need to follow any outbound link to answer
the question as asked.
