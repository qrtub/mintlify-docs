```
https://youtu.be/VIDEO_ID?t=90
```

`?t=90` starts the video 90 seconds in. Drop it to start from the beginning. Use **Share** on the
video to get the short link — it already contains the ID.

<Verified platform="YouTube" date="25 August 2026" />

Free hosting, no app to install, and the person scanning needs no account. For a machine that
needs a start-up procedure or a lockout sequence explained, a video at the right second is often
better than a document.

## Unlisted, not private

- **Unlisted** works — anyone with the link can watch, and it stays out of search and off your
  channel page. This is what a tag wants.
- **Private** does not. It is limited to named Google accounts, so a scanner is refused.
