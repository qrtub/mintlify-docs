Everything about an existing subscription — the card on file, past invoices, canceling — happens in Stripe's billing portal, not in QRtub. Open it from the team page: **Subscription → Manage subscription**. QRtub creates a one-off session for that team's subscription and sends you to Stripe's own site, and Stripe returns you to the team page when you are done.

## It is scoped to one team, and only the owner can open it

The button only exists for the team owner, and the session it creates is for the subscription belonging to the team whose page you opened it from. If you own several paid teams, open the portal from the team you actually mean — or go to **Profile → Your teams & subscriptions** and press **Manage** next to the team you want, which switches you to that team before landing you on its page.

Two other owner-only prompts open the same portal, in a new tab so a half-filled form is not lost: **Change your plan**, which appears beside a plan limit on a team that is already paying, and the top-bar **Upgrade** button on a team whose subscription is unpaid, incomplete or paused.

Asking for the portal on a team that has never been subscribed, or one you do not own, returns an error rather than an empty portal.

## What you can do there

- **Update the payment method** — a new card, or a replacement after one expires.
- **Download invoices and receipts** — the billing history for that team, which is where to go for anything your accountant needs.
- **Cancel the subscription.**
- **Change tier or billing interval**, if the portal is set up to offer it. If you do not see the option, email hi@qrtub.com. Buying again through the in-app plan picker is not a way around it: QRtub refuses a second subscription for a team that Stripe can still bill.

Card details are entered on Stripe's pages throughout. QRtub does not receive or store them.

## What QRtub shows about a scheduled change

QRtub records the team's plan, the status of its subscription, the end of the current billing period, and whether a cancellation or a plan change is scheduled. Invoices and payment details stay in the portal.

A change that takes effect later shows on the Subscription card as a **Scheduled:** line under the plan name, while the current plan keeps working until then:

- After canceling at the end of the billing period: **Scheduled: Set to cancel on** the period's end date, followed by "Your existing codes keep working."
- After a plan change that Stripe schedules for a later date: **Scheduled: Changing to** the new plan **on** its start date, followed by "Change or cancel it with Manage subscription."

When the subscription actually ends, the card shows the plan name followed by `(cancelled)` and the team becomes read-only — see [Subscription Status](/billing/subscription-status).

## After the subscription ends

A canceled team keeps its **Manage subscription** button, which is how you get back in for old invoices. Next to it the card offers **Choose a plan**, which opens the in-app plan picker to subscribe that team again — see [Upgrading a Team's Plan](/billing/upgrade-a-team).

A team whose payment failed (`unpaid`), never completed (`incomplete`) or is paused gets **Manage subscription** only. Stripe can still bring that subscription back, so the way forward is the portal (or hi@qrtub.com, as the card's message says), not a new purchase.
